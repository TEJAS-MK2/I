# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Boli-Khata to be welcoming, respectful and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and professional.
- Assume good intent while giving actionable feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information shared in project discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report concerns privately through GitHub.
